 The Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW), referenced in Charts 4-5, produces comprehensive data on
 the number of establishments, monthly employment, and quarterly wages for workers covered by state
 unemployment insurance (UI) laws, as well as federal workers covered under the Unemployment Compensation for
 Federal Employees (UCFE) program. These data are aggregated across various levels, starting from the 6-digit NAICS
 industry level to broader industry categories (such as NAICS industry groups, sectors, and supersectors) and geographic
 levels (including metropolitan statistical areas, state, and national levels). Based on information reported by New
 Mexico employers, the data cover 97% of nonfarm wage and salary workers and are published quarterly. Renowned
 for their reliability, QCEW data are considered the gold standard for industry statistics, offering the most complete
 universe of monthly employment and quarterly wage information by detailed industry.
